# Product Model

**Updated:** 2026-09-03 — CEO website story redo

## Positioning

**One business meaning. Two ways to work with it.**

Duo Data is Business + Technical, connected by shared semantics (metrics, slices, reports, sources).

The landing page **Experience switch** is the story, not just navigation.

## Users

- Business teams: understand metrics, ask questions, trust answers; improve products/services; post-purchase recommendations; governed meaning when strategy changes; sales intelligence for diverse customers; AI used the right way.
- Data/technology teams: define, govern, version, map, implement the same concepts across the stack.

## Objects (unchanged in app)

Slices, demo requests, mock case studies.

## Funnel

Visitor → choose Business or Technical context → conversation or workspace → demo request.
